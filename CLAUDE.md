# Masar

Masar (مسار, "the path") is a university engagement roadshow for students and parents across Egypt and the Gulf. It is run by GET ED, the official and exclusive study-abroad partner of Eshra7ly (an online education platform with about 27,000 students, 65.8% in the Gulf). It has four formats: fairs, webinars, seminars and outreach. Universities pay to take part: $2,500 for the Fair Pass (one fair), or $6,000 for the Masar Partner package (three fairs, two webinars and seminar access). GET ED counsellors follow up on every lead.

## Visual style

All visual work (decks, web, social, print, illustration) follows `docs/style-guide.md`: loose black brush ink, one oat fill and solid black on a `#F0F0F0` page, with tightly spaced geometric type. Read it before producing or reviewing any visual asset. Items marked *Proposed* in it are still awaiting sign-off from the project owner.
