Chapter IV Masar

Masar مسار

Your gate to university life. Arabic for “the path”.

What Masar is

Not a fair.A guided decision.

Masar is the university engagement platform created by Eshra7ly for the one moment that shapes everything after it: the moment a student decides.

Instead of crowded halls and rushed conversations, Masar walks students and their parents through their options, lets them compare countries, universities and majors side by side, and sends them home with a clear roadmap. No pressure, no hurried choices. Now, with GET ED as its study abroad partner, Masar becomes a roadshow that travels to students across every partner country, all year long.

What Masar sets out to do
  1. Help students make informed university and major choices.
  2. Create meaningful conversations between universities and students.
  3. Bring parents in as the key decision makers they are.
  4. Bridge the gap between school systems and university expectations.
  5. Give universities access to high intent, decision ready students.
  6. Create value long after the event through content, data and follow up.
